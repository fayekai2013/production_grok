# Film Production Knowledge Library v1 (2026-11-18, UTC+8)

This is the substantive knowledge layer behind the film-production bots. Skills stay **routing only** and point here. Each topic folder has:
- `source/`: originals. `PUBLIC_*` = public material with its licence or citation. `OWNER_*` = the owner's own research dossiers, included in full so readers can see exactly what is cited. `THIRDPARTY_*` = public third-party material included in full as attributed citation, each with a `CITATION.md` (author, original URL, retrieval date, rights note). Rights and citations for every item: `RIGHTS_AND_CITATIONS.md`.
- `STUDY_NOTES_EN.md`: our English know-how notes. Each claim is tagged [Source] / [Owner study] / [Ours]. They cover when to use each technique, its tradeoffs and full example prompts.
- `ROLE_MAP.md`: which of the 7 roles uses what. The roles are Lead, Script & Story, Casting & Reference, Previs & Motion, Generation & Edit, Measurement & QC, Recorder & Playbook.

Edition: install-package copy (v1.3, 2026-11-18, UTC+8). Rule (owner ruling 2026-11-18: cited material is included in full, with attribution): wherever a skill, prompt or note cites a tech-radar item (TECH-/LIT-/CON-), the source itself is in this folder, not just its name.

Start at `KNOWLEDGE_ROUTER_EN.md` (problem → topic → source → role). `LIBRARY_INDEX.md` lists the read status and rights class of every item.

Topics: T01 Generative video prompting (The Oath) · T02 Post-production vocabulary (UT 100) · T03 HyperFrames · T04 Motion direction (huashu-motion) · T05 Code-video family & route selection · T06 Measured QC & local editing · T07 Prompting agents: spec vs loop · T08 Field lessons in the skills (pointer) · T09 Adjacent sources.
