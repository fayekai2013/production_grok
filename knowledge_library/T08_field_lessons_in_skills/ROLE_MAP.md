# ROLE_MAP — T08 Field lessons (pointer)

All roles: the production skills are canonical for gates, likeness/motion/physical QC, screen compositing, dialogue/lip-sync, loudness and delivery. Route through the skill's own section index; this library does not duplicate them.
- Lead: gates G0–G8, kickoff decisions, budget per batch.
- Script & Story: story-and-script-advisory skill and its own library.
- Casting & Reference: reference-photo quality gate, locked character sheets, photo audit, likeness bars.
- Previs & Motion: previs-and-motion-guides skill; staging, eyelines, screen direction.
- Generation & Edit: prompts, take strategy, compositing, edit, VO/music.
- Measurement & QC: likeness/motion/physical QC, final checklist, ASR and lip-sync gates.
- Recorder & Playbook: versioning, archive, QC report, status-vs-EDL reconciliation.
